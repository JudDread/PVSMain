Alpha terms and privacy

Project Vacuum State. Last updated 23 October 2026.

This page says, in plain words, what you agree to when you play the alpha test and what happens to your data. If you do not agree, please do not create an account.

Who runs this

Project Vacuum State is made and run by one independent developer. To reach me, use the game’s Discord server: discord.gg/4G8rWeF7AV. That is also where to report bugs and ask for your data to be deleted.

This is an alpha test

  • The game is unfinished. It can break, change or go offline at any time.
  • Your pilot, credits, ships and everything else in the game may be changed or reset, or deleted without notice. Nothing in the game has real-world value.
  • Nothing is sold in the alpha and you pay nothing.

Who may play

You must be 18 or older. By creating an account you confirm that you are.

Playing fair

  • Keep your password to yourself. You are responsible for what happens on your account.
  • Do not attack the game, its servers or other players’ accounts, and do not abuse bugs to damage the game for others. If you find a bug, please report it on Discord.
  • I can remove an account that breaks these rules.

What data I keep

  • Your email address and your password. The password is stored in scrambled form by the login service; I cannot read it.
  • Your pilot name and your game data: credits, ships, cargo, trades, flights, skills, missions, and the times you last acted.
  • The services that host the game (see below) keep ordinary technical logs, such as your IP address when your device connects.

The game page uses no advertising and no tracking cookies. It stores a few settings on your own device, such as your login session and whether you skipped the tutorial tour.

What I do with it

  • I use it only to run and improve the game, for example to fix bugs and balance the economy.
  • I do not sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • Other pilots can see your pilot name and the profile details the game shows. They never see your email address.

Where it is kept

The game page is hosted on GitHub Pages. The database and login service run on Supabase. These companies may store data in other countries. Discord is a separate service with its own rules.

Your choices

  • You can ask on Discord to have your account and all its data deleted. I will do it as soon as I can.
  • You can also ask what data I hold about you.
  • When the alpha ends, I will delete or reset the test data. I will tell you on Discord first if that changes.

No guarantees

The game is provided as it is, with no promise that it works or stays available. To the extent the law allows, I am not liable for losses from playing the alpha. Nothing here limits rights that the law gives you and that cannot be waived.

Changes

I may update this page as the game grows, for example when paid membership is added. The date at the top shows the latest version. Changes that matter will be announced on Discord.
